Home Calendar

A personal calendar for a household wall tablet.

Home Calendar is a small personal project. It runs on a tablet mounted at home and shows the owner's own Google Calendar events next to a to-do list, kitchen timers, a meal plan and a clock.

What it does with Google Calendar

  • Signs in with your Google Account and asks for read-only access to your calendars (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly).
  • Imports your calendars and their events, including titles, times, locations, notes and colors, so they appear on the home screen.
  • Never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar. Data only flows from Google to the app.
  • Keeps the imported events on the owner's own devices at home. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other service.

Who it is for

Home Calendar is a single-user, personal-use application. It is not distributed to the public.
