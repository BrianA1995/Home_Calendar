Home Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Home Calendar ("the app") is a personal, single-user calendar application that runs on devices in the owner's home. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can remove it.

Data the app accesses

If you choose to connect your Google Account, the app requests one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: view the calendars you can access and their events.

Through this permission the app reads your list of calendars (names and colors) and the events in them (titles, start and end times, locations, descriptions and event colors). The app does not request your profile, email address, contacts, files or any other Google data. Calendar names may include your email address, because Google uses it to name your main calendar.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: to display your own events on your own devices. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

  • The app's small server runs on a device in the owner's home. It keeps the imported events and the sign-in credential in a private file on that device.
  • The owner's tablet and phone keep a copy of the imported events so the calendar works offline.
  • The developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and the data is not stored anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. No person other than the owner of the Google Account can read it through the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Imported events are kept until you disconnect your Google Account in the app, which removes them, or you delete the app's data.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer read your calendar.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

You can reach the developer by opening an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this website.
